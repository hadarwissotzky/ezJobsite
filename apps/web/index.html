EZchangeorder

Price a change order on the job site. Text it to the client. Get it signed before the work starts.

How it works

  1. On site, the contractor records what changed — by voice, photo, or typing.
  2. The app writes it up as a scope of work with a price, and the contractor checks and confirms every number before anything is sent.
  3. The client gets a text with a link. They read the scope, see the photos, ask questions if they have any, and approve by signing. No app, no account.
Got a text with an approval link?

Tap the link in your message to read and sign the change order your contractor sent you. Nothing to install and no account needed. If the link has expired, ask your contractor to send it again.
