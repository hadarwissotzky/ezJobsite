What the service does

EZchangeorder lets a residential construction contractor record extra work on a job site, turn it into a priced change order, and send it to their client for review and electronic approval. The client opens a link, reads the scope and price, can ask questions, and can approve or decline.

Text messages

The app sends text messages to clients whose mobile numbers a contractor has entered. By entering a client's number, the contractor confirms that the client gave them that number and expects to be contacted about their project.

Message frequency: event-driven and infrequent — typically a few messages per project. A client receives a message when a change order needs review, at most one reminder per day per change order, and a message if a change order already sent is revised.

Approved change orders are kept as a permanent record and are not edited after signature. That is the point of the product: both sides need to be able to rely on what was agreed. Corrections are made by issuing a new change order, not by rewriting an old one.
