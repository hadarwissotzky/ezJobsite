Delete your account

EZChangeOrders, operated by Hi/Lo Ventures Group · Last updated 11 September 2026

You can delete your EZChangeOrders account and the data that goes with it at any time. You do not need to cancel anything else first, and you do not need to contact us if you can still open the app.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open EZChangeOrders and sign in.
  2. Open the menu.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Close my account.
  4. Read what will be deleted, then tap Delete everything.

Deletion starts immediately and cannot be undone.

Can't open the app? Ask us by email

Email hadar@streamlinesocial.com with the subject Delete my EZChangeOrders account. Send it from the email address on the account, or include the phone number you sign in with, so we can match it to your account. We will reply to confirm once the deletion is complete.

What is deleted

  • Your account details — name, email address, phone number and company name.
  • Your jobs, change orders, prices and notes.
  • Your voice recordings and photos, and the locations stamped on them.
  • Client contact details you entered.
  • Your device's notification token.
  • The copy of all of this stored on your phone.

What is kept

  • A record that each document existed — its date and a cryptographic fingerprint, with no personal details — so an agreement that was already signed can still be verified.
  • Any copy of a change order that a client already received. That copy is theirs.
  • Purchase records held by Apple or Google, under their own policies.

Subscriptions are separate

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription billed by Apple or Google. Cancel it yourself so it does not renew:

  • iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › EZChangeOrders.
  • Android: Google Play Store › your profile icon › Payments and subscriptions › Subscriptions › EZChangeOrders.

Delete some data without closing your account

To delete specific jobs, recordings or photos but keep your account, email hadar@streamlinesocial.com and tell us what to remove.
